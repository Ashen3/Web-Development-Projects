Top Three Activities to do in Falkland

Wildlife Watching

See a massive breeding colony of King penguins (plus Gentoo and Magellanic penguins) via a 4WD trip from Stanley.

Exploring Stanley

Historic Dockyard Museum: Explore local maritime heritage, natural history exhibits, and artifacts from the 1982 Falklands War.

Outdoor & Adventure Activities

Hiking: Trek across open camp landscapes, walk the coastal trails around Stanley, or hike up Mount Usborne (the highest peak) and Mount Longdon.
